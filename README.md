# amplio — Progress, on your terms

A simple, installable wellbeing app prototype. Start with small steps that fit your life, then adjust as life changes.

## Install on a phone

After GitHub Pages is enabled, open the published address on your phone. On iPhone, open it in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

## Demo limits

This prototype has no accounts, coach inbox, or secure server database. Onboarding answers, routine ticks, and check-ins are saved only in the current browser on the current device. Do not enter real customer or health information.
