Add Arclora to your home screen

Arclora is a web app. It opens from a home-screen icon and updates from the website when you open it online.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Arclora in Safari. If this page opened inside another app, first open it in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Turn on Open as Web App if it appears, then tap Add.

Android

  1. Open Arclora in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu and choose Install and create shortcut or Install app.
  3. Follow the prompt to add it to your device.

On some phones, the wording varies by browser. You can still open Arclora directly from its website.
